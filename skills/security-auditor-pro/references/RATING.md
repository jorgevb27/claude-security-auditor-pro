# Internal Security Rating (not third-party certification)
Score 0–100 from six dimensions, each scored 0–100: code 20%, identity 20%, DB 15%, infrastructure/TLS 15%, APIs 15%, controlled validation 15%. Exclude untested dimensions from a *verified* score: do not silently assume a perfect result. Report provisional score with assessed-weight percentage and coverage separately. If less than 80% of weighted dimensions are assessed, do not award stars; label insufficient coverage. With >=80% assessed: 0–39 one star, 40–59 two, 60–79 three, 80–94 four, 95–100 five. Cap at two stars for an unresolved confirmed critical issue; cap at three for an unresolved confirmed high issue. Five stars require no confirmed unresolved critical/high findings, >=95% assessed-weight coverage, and recent evidence. Report evidence freshness and confidence. Score is a prioritization aid, not proof of safety.
